1. Product Vision
   Studdy is a multi-tutor platform designed primarily for independent tutors.
   It helps tutors manage bookings, lesson preparation, student progress, homework, payments,
   assessments

and

teaching

resources

in

one

connected

system.

Parents gain a clear understanding of where their child is progressing, where support is needed and
what

their

tutor

is

doing

to

help.

Students remain the central beneficiaries of the platform. Studdy focuses first on giving tutors the tools,
context

and

organisation

required

to

support

each

student

effectively.

2. Core Product Principle
   Better tutor visibility leads to better student support and greater parent trust.
   Studdy should help tutors understand:
   ● What is happening next ● What happened previously ● Where the student is progressing ● Where the student is struggling ● What work remains outstanding ● What parents and students need ● What action the tutor should take next
3. Primary Users
   Studdy serves three main user groups.
   Tutors
   Tutors are the primary operational users.
   Studdy should help tutors:
   ● Manage bookings and availability ● Prepare for lessons ● Record lesson outcomes ● Track student progress and concerns ● Assign and review homework ● Manage payments ● Create and deliver assessments

<!-- page 2 -->

● Access and contribute teaching resources ● Communicate with families ● Build a trusted professional profile
Parents and Guardians
Parents should be able to:
● Find and evaluate tutors ● Request bookings ● Manage multiple children ● View homework and approved lesson summaries ● Understand progress at a glance ● Access detailed progress when needed ● Track payments, packages and upcoming lessons ● Record tests, exams and important school dates ● Suggest changes to goals and progress records ● Raise support or safety concerns
Students
Students should receive an age-appropriate experience.
Dependent students may receive permissions from a parent, such as the ability to request bookings, add test dates or submit homework.
Independent students should receive parent-equivalent control over their own account, bookings, payments, goals, progress and records. 4. Platform Positioning
Studdy will operate as:
A tutor operating system
A central place for tutors to manage their tutoring work, students, bookings, payments, preparation, records and resources.
A curated tutor network
Tutors apply to join the Studdy network and are interviewed, verified and approved before offering services through the platform.
A trusted tutor marketplace
Parents can discover approved tutors, view their profiles, compare suitability and request bookings.
Studdy may recommend tutors based on:
● Subject and year-level suitability ● Availability ● Ratings ● Reliability ● Location or online format ● Parent preferences

● Fair exposure for new tutors
Parents can also browse beyond the recommended tutors. 5. Tutor Independence
Tutors remain independent providers.
They should be able to control:
● Availability ● Subjects ● Year levels ● Pricing ● Discounts ● Lesson packages ● Online and in-person offerings ● Student capacity ● Travel limits ● Communication channels ● Whether they offer instant booking ● Whether they accept new students ● Which student segments they currently accept
Studdy may provide suggested pricing, platform standards and configurable operating rules.
Studdy receives a percentage of revenue generated from bookings and paid services managed through the platform. 6. Student-Centred Learning Continuity
Studdy should create a continuous learning record for each student.
The student profile should bring together:

1. Next lesson 2. Progress 3. Homework 4. Goals 5. Concerns 6. Recent lessons
   Progress should support:
   ● Curriculum standards ● Tutor-created skills ● Tutor judgement ● Assessment evidence ● Completed work ● Parent and student input
   Parents should receive a simple progress overview by default, with a detailed mode available.

7. Lesson Experience
   Before a lesson, tutors should receive a briefing containing:
   ● Recent lesson summary ● Current goals ● Progress and concerns ● Homework status ● Upcoming tests and deadlines ● Parent or student comments ● Relevant resources ● Previous next-lesson plan
   Tutors should be able to store a next-lesson plan after each lesson.
   Later, Studdy may suggest the next lesson plan using AI for tutor review.
   Online lessons should eventually take place within an embedded Studdy lesson room.
   Until then, Studdy may connect to an external video platform such as Google Meet.
   Lesson recordings should be standard for online lessons, subject to appropriate consent and privacy controls.
   Studdy should generate:
   ● A transcript ● An AI overview ● Suggested homework ● Suggested progress updates ● Suggested next steps
   The tutor must review and approve the AI-generated overview before it becomes part of the student record.
   Parents receive only the approved summary and homework.
   Raw recordings should be retained for approximately three days, with the exact retention period configurable by admin.
8. Homework and Assessments
   Studdy should support both formal homework submission and flexible lesson-based review.
   Students may:
   ● Upload work ● Type answers ● Submit links ● Mark work complete ● Review unfinished work during a lesson
   Tutors may create formal assessments that can be:
   ● Completed live ● Completed independently

<!-- page 5 -->

● Combined with a follow-up lesson ● Sold as part of an assessment package
Parents and independent students may browse and purchase assessments.
Studdy may also create and sell its own assessments.
Assessment results may:
● Update progress records ● Identify learning gaps ● Suggest goals ● Recommend resources ● Recommend suitable tutors ● Generate an AI draft report for tutor approval 9. Resource Community and Marketplace
Studdy should be community-driven.
Tutors should be encouraged to upload, review and improve teaching resources.
Resources may be:
● Free community resources ● Paid premium resources ● Tutor-created ● Studdy-created ● AI-assisted ● Curriculum-aligned ● Peer reviewed ● Subject specialist reviewed
Tutors should retain ownership while granting Studdy the rights needed to store, moderate, display and distribute the resource within the platform.
Tutors may fork and republish community resources when the new version adds meaningful value and preserves attribution.
All paid resources require admin approval.
Contributors may earn:
● Platform credits ● Badges ● Visibility ● Contributor status ● Premium-resource access ● Reduced platform fees at defined milestones
Parents and students may access tutor-assigned resources. Self-selected resources may require payment.

10. Tutor Trust and Profiles
    Tutor profiles should display:
    ● Profile photo ● Identity verification ● Interview status ● Reference-check status ● Verified qualifications where claimed ● Subjects and year levels ● Experience ● Teaching approach ● Availability ● Pricing ● Ratings and reviews ● Completed lessons ● Resource contributions ● Specialist-reviewer status ● Introductory meeting availability
    Qualifications are optional.
    False or misleading qualifications are unacceptable.
    Tutors may include capable secondary-school students, university students, experienced tutors, qualified teachers and subject specialists.
    Tutors may offer only subjects and year levels they have added to their profile. Areas may carry labels such as self-declared, experience verified, qualification verified or Studdy reviewed.
11. Ratings and Transparency
    Parent ratings should be public and multidimensional.
    Possible rating categories include:
    ● Teaching quality ● Communication ● Reliability ● Student engagement ● Clarity ● Preparation ● Parent confidence ● Homework usefulness ● Professionalism
    Ratings should be visible by subject where enough data exists.
    Tutors may dispute ratings.
    Admin may remove reviews that are abusive, fraudulent, irrelevant or reveal inappropriate personal information.
    New tutors should display a clear “New to Studdy” status rather than appearing poorly rated due to limited history.

Students may provide lightweight private feedback after lessons. 12. Booking and Capacity
The default booking process should be:

1. Parent or independent student requests a lesson. 2. Tutor reviews the student’s needs. 3. Tutor accepts or declines. 4. Booking becomes confirmed. 5. If declined, Studdy suggests alternative tutors.
   Tutors may later enable instant booking as an optional setting.
   Tutors should be able to manage capacity by:
   ● Subject ● Year level ● Online or in-person format ● Location ● Day and time ● New versus existing students
   Tutors should be able to stop accepting new students entirely while continuing with existing students.
   Tutors may maintain waiting lists.
   Waiting lists should be first come, first served among families whose availability matches newly opened capacity.
   Parents may join several waiting lists at once.
2. Payments
   The initial multi-tutor payment model should be:
3. Parent pays the tutor directly. 2. Studdy records the charge and payment status. 3. Studdy calculates commission from completed or chargeable bookings. 4. Studdy charges the tutor separately for commission owed. 5. Continued commission non-payment may result in platform suspension.
   Studdy should support:
   ● Prepaid lessons ● Postpaid lessons ● Packages paid upfront ● Packages paid lesson by lesson ● Discounts ● Family payments ● Multiple authorised payers ● Credits ● Refunds ● Partial payments ● Payment reconciliation

The standard postpaid deadline should initially be three calendar days after the lesson, with the exact period configurable by admin.
Every parent, student, family, tutor, booking and payment should have a permanent ID.
For current bank transfers, each family should have one stable payment reference.
Studdy should connect to Darsha’s ASB account through Akahu for automatic payment matching.
Payment matching should use:
● Family reference ● Payer name ● Recognised source account details ● Amount ● Outstanding family balance ● Expected timing ● Payment history
Lesson completion and payment status must remain separate. 14. In-Person Lessons
Parents may choose:
● Their home ● Tutor’s approved location ● Public location ● No preference
Tutors can accept or decline based on location, travel and suitability.
Travel fees should use a standard Studdy rate based on one-way driving time.
Tutors may configure:
● Nearby free zone ● Standard travel zone ● Extended travel zone ● Maximum travel time ● Unavailable areas
Tutor home addresses remain private until relevant and approved for a booking.
Parent addresses remain private from the public and visible only where required for a booking.
Parents may save multiple addresses.
Parents must disclose pets at home locations, including type, size, behaviour and whether the animal can be secured.
Tutors may make pet-related decisions case by case.

15. Safety, Privacy and Admin Access
    Studdy should provide authorised admin with access to bookings, lesson records, payments, messages, recordings, transcripts and student records when required for:
    ● Support ● Safeguarding ● Moderation ● Quality assurance ● Payment administration ● Legal obligations
    Sensitive access should be logged.
    Users should be clearly informed that platform communications may be reviewed when appropriate.
    Tutors and parents may block each other after serious issues.
    Safety reports may temporarily pause upcoming bookings.
    Admin may apply warnings, targeted restrictions, suspensions or permanent bans.
    Incident-retention periods should be configurable through the admin dashboard.
    The Studdy privacy officer will initially be the platform administrator.
16. Admin-First Configurability
    Routine operational decisions should be manageable through the admin interface wherever practical.
    Admin should eventually be able to configure:
    ● Commission rates ● Suggested pricing ● Cancellation rules ● Rescheduling rules ● Payment deadlines ● Tutor status ● Tutor visibility ● Subjects and year levels ● Resource approvals ● Rating categories ● Homepage content ● Notification templates ● Feature access ● Retention periods ● Recommendation rules ● Capacity rules ● Travel pricing ● Payment matching ● Platform restrictions
    Major structural or technical changes may still require code.

17. Product Design Principles
    Studdy should follow these principles:
    Tutor-first operation
    The platform should reduce tutor administration and improve teaching preparation.
    Student-centred outcomes
    Features should ultimately improve student support, continuity and understanding.
    Parent clarity
    Parents should understand what is happening, what progress is being made and what action is needed.
    Trust through transparency
    Profiles, ratings, pricing, qualifications, resource authorship and platform rules should be clear.
    Configurable over hard-coded
    Operational choices should be manageable by admin and, where appropriate, by tutors.
    Human review of AI
    AI may draft, summarise and recommend. Tutors or authorised admins approve consequential records.
    Community before extraction
    Studdy should encourage useful contribution, shared resources and tutor growth.
    Fair tutor opportunity
    Recommendation systems should balance suitability, trust, availability and exposure for new tutors.
    Privacy with accountability
    Sensitive information should be protected, while authorised access remains possible for support and safeguarding.
    Flexible growth
    The architecture should support the current personal tutoring operation while preparing for a multi-tutor platform.
