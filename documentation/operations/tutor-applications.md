# Reviewing tutor applications — the reviewer's how-to

For Platform Managers and Owners. Open **Tutor applications** in the manager workspace
(`/manager/tutor-applications`). You will need your MFA code: the page and every action on it
re-check both your role and your MFA session.

The decisions behind this, and the ones still open, are in
`docs/decisions/tutor-onboarding-decisions.md`.

---

## 1. The queue

Oldest first. **Needs review** is applications waiting on you. **With the applicant** is those
where you asked for changes. Each shows how many of the four checks are verified.

## 2. Read the application

It shows the applicant's legal name, the name families will see, their sign-in email (so you can
reach them), what they teach, and their referees. Studdy does not email referees: contact them
yourself.

## 3. Record the checks

Four checks, each recorded on its own: **identity**, **safeguarding requirements**,
**references** and **interview**. Do each one outside the form and record the outcome here:
**Mark verified**, **Mark failed**, or **Reset**. Add a note if it helps; the note is yours and
staff-only, and the applicant never sees it. Studdy keeps the outcome, not documents: do not
paste an identity document or number into a note.

Recording your first check moves the application to **Under review**.

## 4. Decide

- **Approve.** Enabled only when every required check is verified. It creates the tutor's
  profile and workspace in one step. The tutor is **not** visible to families until they have a
  published service, which is reviewed separately.
- **Request changes.** Write a message for the applicant; they read it exactly as written, edit
  their application and resubmit it as a new revision.
- **Decline.** Write a message for the applicant. Do not include anything about other people.

Both messages are shown to the applicant, so write them for them. Your checks and notes are not.

## If Approve says it is not ready

It names the checks that are still to verify and any that failed. A failed check blocks approval:
either the applicant has changes to make (request them), or the answer is no (decline).

## If approval is refused as "already a tutor"

This person already has a tutor profile. Do not go on; find out why before doing anything else.
